OBSERVATIONAL NOTE // EXPLANATION VOL. XLII

Spanning approximately 200,000 light-years across, NGC 1365 is an enormous barred spiral galaxy lying within the southern constellation Fornax. Majestic dust lanes sweep through a prominent central stellar bar, funneling raw interstellar gas straight into the galactic core where a supermassive black hole spins at nearly the theoretical limit of relativity.

High-energy X-ray observations and infrared spectroscopy suggest violent star formation rings lining the bar's inner orbital resonance. The colossal gravity of the bar produces turbulent gas shocks, sparking clusters of brilliant, sapphire-hot young stellar systems along its serpentine spiral ribbons.
